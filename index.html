Frequently asked questions

    
First time commissioning? No worries, there are no silly questions.

    
What is Live2D?
Live2D makes a 2D character move: blinking, talking, and turning its head, usually with a face-tracking camera while streaming. It comes in two parts: the model art (the layered drawing) and the rigging (what makes it move).

    
Do I need a finished character design?
It helps, but it’s not required. Reference images, a written description, or a moodboard are all good starting points.

    
How does ordering and payment work?
Choose the service you’d like, then send me a DM on X. We’ll talk through the details and confirm the cost again before anything starts. Once we’ve agreed, I’ll begin working, and I’ll share progress updates on X at each stage, such as sketch, base, rendering, and rigging. Prefer Discord? Just let me know on X.
